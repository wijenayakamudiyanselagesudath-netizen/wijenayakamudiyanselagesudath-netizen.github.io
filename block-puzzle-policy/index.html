This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you install and use Block Puzzle Terracotta (the "App").

For a better experience, while using our App, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information. The information that we request will be retained on your device and is not collected by us in any way, except for the services listed below.

The App uses Firebase services (like Firestore and Analytics) to manage leaderboards and improve user experience. This may involve collecting:

  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the app.

The App uses AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data (such as your device's advertising ID) to show personalized ads.

c. Local Storage

The App uses local storage (Room database and SharedPreferences) to save your game progress, high scores, and settings locally on your device.
